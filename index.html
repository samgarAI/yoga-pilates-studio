Body Practice Directions

Classified Studio Practices

Each direction is structured by preparation gradation, thermal comfort, and session duration to match your personal rhythm.

Hatha Yoga

Foundational static postures (asanas), pranayama breathwork, and joint mobility sequences designed to restore nervous system equilibrium and postural alignment.

  • Level: Gentle / All Levels
  • Timing: 60 min (08:00 & 18:00)
Book Hatha Yoga Hall

Hot Yoga

Dynamic detoxifying flow conducted in a humidified infrared hall at 36°C–38°C to accelerate myofascial release, cardiovascular endurance, and deep flexibility.

  • Level: High Intensity (38°C)
  • Timing: 75 min (09:30 & 19:30)
Book Hot Yoga Hall

Pilates

Precision core stabilization and spinal decompression on balanced wooden Reformers and anatomical mats, cultivating deep muscular control without joint impact.

  • Level: Adaptive Spring Load
  • Timing: 55 min (07:30 & 18:30)
Book Pilates Hall
